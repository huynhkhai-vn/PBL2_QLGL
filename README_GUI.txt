PBL2 - GIAO DIEN DO HOA RAYLIB

1. Giai nen toan bo thu muc.
2. Mo thu muc 'PBL2 - Copy' bang VS Code.
3. Dam bao Raylib da duoc cai trong MSYS2 UCRT64 va lenh g++ co the tim thay raylib.h.
4. Chay build_gui.bat tu thu muc goc project.

Loi bien dich do ten mau BLUE/GREEN/RED/YELLOW trung voi macro he thong da duoc khac phuc:
 cac hang mau rieng duoc doi ten thanh PBL_BLUE, PBL_GREEN, PBL_RED, PBL_YELLOW, ...

Neu van bao loi khong tim thay raylib.h hoac -lraylib, hay kiem tra ban dang dung terminal MSYS2 UCRT64 va Raylib da duoc cai dat.

Giao dien:
- Nut 'Doi diem di' / 'Doi diem den' de chuyen giao lo.
- Nut tim duong de chay Dijkstra.
- Nut mo phong de taxi di theo tuyen da tim.
- Nut xoa ket qua de lam lai.
- ESC de dong cua so.

Chay tu thu muc goc de chuong trinh doc dung Data/intersections.txt va Data/roads.txt.
